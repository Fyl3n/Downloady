# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Download a video or its audio from a pasted link, or from the page open in
  Safari or a Chromium browser, with the quality, container and audio format
  you choose.
- A queue you can leave: progress in the live activity, a HUD when the file
  lands, Cancel removes what was half written.
- Playlists become one download per entry; live streams are recorded until
  you stop them.
- Subtitles from the site, or a transcript made on this Mac (macOS 26).
- Keyboard shortcuts for downloading the current tab or the pasted link.
- yt-dlp, ffmpeg and Deno from your Mac or installed by Downloady, checked
  against their published checksums.
